Collection — new action-button style

Pixel-stepped corners on the top-left + bottom-right only (TR + BL stay square), on every action button. Flat 2D — no raised edge, no bevel, nothing moves on press. Pressed/selected combines both effects: the body gets Scanline Energize (fine CRT scanlines over a hairline-darkened fill) and the two stepped corners fill orange (--scr-accent); the pip is removed. Specimen for sign-off before it goes on the board.
