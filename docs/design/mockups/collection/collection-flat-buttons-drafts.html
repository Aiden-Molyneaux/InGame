Collection · Shelf — Flat-Button Drafts (A · B · C)

The Shelf main page, three ways to make the on-screen controls read 2D / non-3D. Every draft removes the raised keycap edge and the press-down motion; what differs is the pressed/selected affordance: A inverts the fill, B recesses it (depth, no movement), C rings the perimeter. Scope = in-screen controls only — LOG HOURS, the sort / filter / view chips, and ADD; the physical nav keys below the screen stay as the device's hardware (unchanged). The sort chip shows each draft's pressed state live, and the legend strip under every device shows IDLE vs PRESSED side by side.

Flat-button drafts same Shelf main page, three distinct 2D pressed-state treatments — no raised keycap edge, nothing moves on press. In-screen controls only (LOG HOURS · the sort/filter/view chips · ADD); the physical nav keys stay hardware. Hold any button, or watch the live sort chip + the legend strip under each device.

Draft A — tonal inversion

Idle: clean flat fills, no edge, no bevel. Pressed / selected: the fill and the ink swap places — the cap floods to its dark ink with light type, the chip floods navy with a cream glyph. A hard colour flip, zero motion. The loudest of the three; unmistakable even at a glance, and it doubles as a clear "this filter is on" marker on the chips.

Draft B — inset recess

Idle: the same flat fills. Pressed / selected: the fill darkens a step and an inner shadow sinks the surface in — depth without movement. The cap stays exactly where it is; only its light flips from "raised" to "pushed in." Quietest of the three and the most tactile; it keeps the original keycap feel but with nothing physically displacing.

Draft C — outline ring

Idle: the same flat fills. Pressed / selected: a crisp accent ring hugs the perimeter (just inside the edge, so it follows the C5 corner-notch) and the fill brightens a touch — the affordance lives on the edge, not the surface, so the button never changes shape or shifts. Leans hardest into the arcade chrome; the ring reads as a clean "focused / armed" state and stays legible even where a fill-only change would be too subtle.
