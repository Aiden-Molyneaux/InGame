C5 · Hybrid — square chrome, stepped-diagonal cards

System: chrome radius 0 + 1px hairline panels (from C3) · cards clip TL + BR with the pixel-step (4/8px double-step at hero size, 3px single-notch at mini, 2.5px at thumb) and keep TR + BL at 90°; art inset and plate repeat the diagonal at smaller steps. Rank chips and all other chrome stay square — the step belongs to cards only, making them the single shaped object in the system.

Watch: the diagonal must never flip (TL + BR always stepped) or cards read as misprinted; and because the step is the card's signature, the device editor / store tiles should avoid stepped corners so nothing competes with it.
