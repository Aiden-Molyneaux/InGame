SectionSwitch — 3 takes for a new mode-switcher (replaces the Segmented Toolbar)

Owner picked Device Draft A and asked to replace the Segmented Toolbar convention — A's SectionChips (SHELL · THEME · STICKERS), kin to Discover's SegmentedKeycap (UP NEXT ↔ DISCOVER) and the Styler's SectionChips (the 5 carousel sections) — with a component modeled on the options in Settings S8 (the TriageCard: an icon tile + title + one-line description). The tension: S8's cards pick-once-and-route; a tool switcher is always present and must coexist with the active section's controls. The three takes answer that differently. Selection moves to the F-09 language — scr.accent border + pink ChipPip (not the old F-03 pressed-keycap tell). Shown in the Device A context; each caption notes how it generalizes to the 2-way (Discover) and 5-way (Styler) cases. Working names SectionSwitch / SectionCard — flagged at the gate; cross-app adoption on the converged Discover/Styler boards is a separate spec-owner call (logged). Tokens verbatim.

T1a — Section Stack (STICKERS open)

The most faithful S8 adaptation: the switcher is a vertical list of SectionCards (icon + title + one-line subtitle). The active card carries the accent border + pink pip and expands in place — its tray nests inside the open card; the others fold to compact rows. Most legible & self-describing of the three.

Generalizes: 2-way (Discover) → two tall cards, the open one unfurls its content — roomy but heavy for a frequent toggle. 5-way (Styler) → the weak case: five cards is a tall list; only viable if collapsed rows are slim.

T2a — Section Rail (STICKERS active)

The SectionCard DNA compressed to a compact icon-card rail — icon + label, all sections always visible, the active one with the accent border + pip. The active section's tray docks beneath the rail. Closest to the old Segmented Toolbar's footprint, but richer (icons + labels, not bare keycaps) and on the F-09 selection language.

Generalizes cleanly: 2-way (Discover) → two wide cards, bottom-docked (basically today's SegmentedKeycap, upgraded). 5-way (Styler) → the strong case: five icon-cards fit on one rail (scroll if needed), tray below — the carousel's jump rail.

T2b — Section Rail (switched to THEME)

Tapping THEME just re-aims the dock — the rail holds its place, the pip jumps, and the tray swaps to the theme swatches (the whole-page DEV-04 preview rides as it did in A). The switch is a single tap with no layout reflow, the lowest-friction of the three for a tool you flip often.

T3a — Section Hero (STICKERS, paged)

Only the active section shows — as a rich hero card (big icon + title + subtitle + its tray inline), the neighbours peeking at the edges. You swipe or tap ‹ › to page between sections; the elongated accent dot + the name strip mark where you are. Kin to the Styler carousel, but the SECTION itself is the card.

Generalizes: 2-way (Discover) → a two-page swipe (UP NEXT ↔ DISCOVER) — clean but heavier than a tap-toggle. 5-way (Styler) → natural: it already is the carousel; the section-hero is just a richer section header. Trade-off: only one section visible at a time (the others are a swipe away, not a glance).

T3b — Section Hero (swiped to THEME)

Swipe left and the hero pages to THEME (dot 2, count 2/3) — same rich card, new content (the theme swatches; the whole-page DEV-04 preview rides on KEEP). The peeking neighbours promise SHELL one swipe back, STICKERS one swipe on. Beautiful and descriptive; the cost is you only ever see one section at a glance.

At a glance — pick one to fold into Device A (then it can propagate)

T1 Section Stack — most legible/descriptive; best when sections are few and space is free; weakest at 5-way. T2 Section Rail — the compact upgrade to the Segmented Toolbar; lowest-friction switching, no reflow; scales cleanly to 2-way (Discover) and 5-way (Styler) — the safe, convention-respecting choice. T3 Section Hero — the richest active state and a natural fit for the Styler carousel, at the cost of only one section visible at a time.

Scope: built in the Device A context only. Adopting the winner on the converged Discover (SegmentedKeycap) and Styler (SectionChips) boards is a cross-track / spec-owner decision — logged as OQ-063, not applied here. Working names SectionSwitch / SectionCard ratify into the catalog only at converge.
