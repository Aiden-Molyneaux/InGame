Discover — Draft A “Two rooms” (hard-binary toggle · clarity pole)

The UP NEXT ↔ DISCOVER toggle is a hard binary — two purpose-built screens that share nothing but the control. Up Next is a focused, legible management list (Now Playing pinned, drag-to-reorder, source-tagged rows). Discover is a sectioned browse hub (Upcoming rail · genre/studio chips · Trending peek · search). Switching is a room-switch — the whole content swaps. The clarity/separation pole of §1.1.

SegmentedKeycap/pair: two keycaps, hard seam, active pressed + pink PipLight — the plain, most-conventional segmented control. The on-screen header IS the control (no h2 title); the plastic DISCOVER keycap carries tab identity. No PIXELS mark — Discover is non-commerce (trending shows adoption counts, not prices). Lifecycle (Skeleton · “Signal Lost”+RETRY · Offline writes-gated, SYS-10) + reduce-motion/non-gesture notes deferred to converge. Values illustrative (OQ-002/011).
