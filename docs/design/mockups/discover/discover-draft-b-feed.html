Discover — Draft B “One feed, lensed” (toggle = a lens · continuity pole)

Up Next and Discover are facets of one continuous scroll. The toggle is a LENS, not a room — UP NEXT narrows the feed to your queue, DISCOVER widens the same feed to the catalog. The bands blend — queue → friend recs → upcoming → trending, one river on a left flow-rail. The lens changes emphasis/width, never the surface. The continuity pole of §1.1.

SegmentedKeycap/lens: ONE seated keycap (pressed, F-03) sliding in a track — it bears the aperture glyph + the pink PipLight and an accent under-fill; the other label waits on the bare track. A lens cue (◐ queue / ◯ catalog) narrates the focus width. No PIXELS mark (non-commerce). Lifecycle (Skeleton · “Signal Lost”+RETRY · Offline writes-gated, SYS-10) + reduce-motion/non-gesture notes deferred to converge. Values illustrative (OQ-002/011).
