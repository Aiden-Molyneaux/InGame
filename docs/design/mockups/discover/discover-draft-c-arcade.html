Discover — Draft C “Arcade aisles” (diegetic · metaphor pole)

The spatial/diegetic take (the C-line from the styler/canvas tracks). Up Next = a physical cartridge load-queue — cards are cartridges in a magazine you slide to reslot; the Now Playing pin is the loaded slot. Discover = walking the arcade floor — an Upcoming marquee overhead, genre aisles you step into, and a Trending cabinet. The toggle is a hardware lever on the device. The metaphor pole of §1.1.

SegmentedKeycap/lever: a diegetic two-position selector worn on-screen — a recessed throw-track with two detents and a ridged knob seated in the active one (pressed, F-03) carrying the active label + the pink PipLight. Pull it QUEUE ⇄ ARCADE. Stays on the screen (F-04 untouched). No PIXELS mark (non-commerce; the cabinet shows adoption high-scores, not prices). Lifecycle (Skeleton · “Signal Lost”+RETRY · Offline writes-gated, SYS-10) + reduce-motion/non-gesture notes deferred to converge. Values illustrative (OQ-002/011).
