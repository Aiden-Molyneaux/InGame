Game page — Draft A “Card-led scroll” (4.2 · the adaptive hub)

The card object is the hero — full-face at the top, tap-flips front↔back (back = auto-stats + provenance, CARD-01) — and the whole page is one prioritized vertical scroll beneath it. The page adapts to how you arrived: the band directly under the card changes — neutral (ADD TO COLLECTION) · owned (your inline-edit `PlayStats`) · friend-view (their `EquipReadout` + ADOPT, SOC-11). Card-centric, everything-visible — the InGame-native pole. Hosts the community gallery + adopt (atomic, ECON-03/04) and the card switcher (the OQ-056 customizations view: your cards for this game + design-new → Styler). Lifecycle cells (soft-hidden · loading · offline) deferred to converge. Sample: Destiny, 210 hrs; Maverick = you · Riko/Vanta = friends. Numbers illustrative (OQ-002/011).

P3 — The card flip (the card-object lives here, §4.2)

The Game page hosts the card-object/flip: tap the hero and it turns to its standardized back (CARD-01) — your per-game stats auto-filled up top, then the card's provenance: DESIGNED BY MAVERICK · ADOPTED 12× · the equipped cosmetics · the “made in InGame” mark.
