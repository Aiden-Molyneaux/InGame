Game page — Draft C “Pinned card + drawer depth” (4.2 · the adaptive hub)

The card pins compact at the top with its primary action always present — it never leaves your sight — while the body is a lean summary: a few hairline-separated rows, lots of open space, almost no boxes. The heavy content lives on demand in drawers (the app's one bottom-sheet grammar): YOUR PLAY (stats) · STATS & PROVENANCE (the card-back) · YOUR CARDS (switcher) · COMMUNITY CARDS (gallery + adopt) · COMPARE. The sheet-family pole — the airiest take, the fewest-containers take (your note). The page adapts the pin's strip + primary action: neutral → ADD · owned → EDIT + your stat strip · friend → ADOPT. In C the flip is a drawer too — the front never leaves the pin; tap it and the back (auto-stats + provenance, CARD-01) rises as a sheet. Lifecycle (soft-hidden · loading · offline) deferred to converge. Sample: Destiny, 210 hrs; Maverick = you · Riko/Vanta = friends. Numbers illustrative (OQ-002/011).
