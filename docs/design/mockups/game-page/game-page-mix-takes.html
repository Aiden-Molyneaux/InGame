Game page — the A×B mix (large card + a bottom dossier switch) · 3 takes

Your gate notes on draft A: embrace B's dossier effect but keep the card as large as A · add a delete affordance for your saved cards · make the dossier fit the design system · bring the dossier tab to the bottom of the screen. The key move on the last two: the bottom section-switch is the `SegmentedKeycap` — the same component the converged Discover board docked at the bottom (now design-spec §1.5), widened from a 2-up pair to a 3-up PLAY · CARDS · ABOUT trio (keycap physics F-03, active = pressed + pink pip). The dossier body itself is just the app's standard list-row grammar (label · value, hairline-ruled, square C5) — nothing newly invented. These three takes isolate the owned dossier (the richest context); neutral · friend-view · upcoming carry from A rev 4 unchanged and aren't re-drawn here. Each take differs only in how the large card relates to the bottom-tabbed dossier. Sample: Destiny, 210 hrs; Maverick = you. Numbers illustrative (OQ-002/011). Delete semantics flagged → OQ-061.
