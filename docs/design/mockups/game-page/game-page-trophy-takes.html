Game page — “Card as trophy” · 3 fresh takes

Brainstorm outcome (2026-06-14): the page's primary job on your own game is the card as a trophy. So all three takes make the equipped card the unquestioned star and give every other piece of the §4.2 inventory a calm, clearly-subordinate home — the cure for the earlier “incoherence” (too many things sharing equal weight). Same design system throughout (Teal shell · Midnight · C5 · `GameCard` · `SegmentedKeycap` · the sheet grammar · `KeycapButton`). Each take is a different interaction model for the same trophy: A the card collapses into a sticky header as you scroll · B the card is full-bleed and gesture-operated · C the card is the heart of a glanceable dashboard. Shown on the owned state (richest); neutral/friend-view reuse each take's spine. Delete affordance included (OQ-061). Sample: Destiny, 210 hrs; Maverick = you. Numbers illustrative (OQ-002/011).

A2 — Scrolled (the card becomes a header; the inventory unfolds)

Once you scroll, the trophy collapses to a slim header (mini face + title + equipped readout + Now) that stays pinned — the card never disappears, but yields the screen to depth. Below, calm prioritized sections: Your Play (glance + EDIT→sheet) · Your Cards (rail + MANAGE/delete, equipped locked) · Community (adopt peek) · Friends & About. Every §4.2 item has one obvious home. This is the iOS large-title / Wallet pattern — predictable, coherent.
