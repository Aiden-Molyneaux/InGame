Feedback & bug reporting (SYS-11) — Draft A “Inline expanding row”

One of three distinct interaction models for the SYS-11 feedback surface. The distinctness axis = how you get from the Settings row to a filed report. Draft A's answer: you don't go anywhere — the FEEDBACK & BUG REPORTING row expands in place.

Thesis — zero-navigation, the form comes to you

Tapping FEEDBACK & BUG REPORTING discloses a compact form right inside the FEEDBACK & SUPPORT `Well` (the rows below push down); you pick a type (`TypePick` — 3 inline segmented keycaps), type a message, and SEND without ever leaving Settings. Fast and low-ceremony — ideal for the common "quick note" case.

MUST PROVE: when type = BUG, the device-logs control (`LogAttach`) unfurls inline — and it has to fit the cramped footprint without feeling squeezed or hiding the consent line. A2 is that proof: a compact toggle row + a always-visible consent line + a single reserved OQ-060 payload chip.

Stage 1 — In place, in Settings A1 the row expanded at rest (type FEEDBACK · empty · SEND dormant) · A2 type = BUG → the `LogAttach` unfurls inline with the logs toggle ON (the proof: consent line never hidden, payload room reserved) · A3 SEND held pressed (in-flight)

A1 — idle (expanded in place · type FEEDBACK · SEND dormant)

Zero navigation. The FEEDBACK row's chevron flips to ▾ and the form discloses inside the same `Well` (orange-tinted to read as the row's child) — HELP & CONTACT just pushes down. `TypePick` = 3 segmented keycaps (FEEDBACK selected = pressed + pink pip, the SegmentedKeycap grammar); a short `TextField/area`; the red SEND sits dormant until there's a message.

This is the whole point of Draft A: a quick note never costs a screen change. The non-bug case stays tiny — no logs control appears unless you choose BUG (A2).

A2 — bug + logs attached (the proof · `LogAttach` inline)

Choosing BUG unfurls `LogAttach` in place. The compact treatment is the test of Draft A: a single panel carrying (a) the ATTACH INGAME LOGS row with the square `Toggle` ON (orange), (b) the consent line, full-width and never clipped — "support team only, never shown to other players, off by default", and (c) the reserved OQ-060 payload slot: an "INGAME DIAGNOSTIC LOGS · ATTACHED" chip whose dashed sub-row holds the room where bundle size + contents will render once the format is defined, plus WHAT'S INCLUDED?.

The transient surface is the converged `Toast` sliding under the header — COULDN'T SEND YOUR REPORT · NOTHING WAS LOST — YOUR MESSAGE IS STILL HERE, with the orange RETRY (safe to repeat). Error, not offline: offline would gate SEND calmly with no red (the Settings page's OfflineStrip pattern, SYS-10) — this red is reserved for a true failure.
