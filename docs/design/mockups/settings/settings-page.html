Settings — the conventional page (§4.15) + the full page-state matrix

Settings is a well-trodden pattern, so it's built once, composed straight from the design-spec §1.5 catalog: a `FlowTakeover` reached from Profile's gear (`FlowHeader` ◂ + "SETTINGS"; the device frame + `NavBand` persist, PROFILE keycap pressed). Sections are `Well`s of `ListRow`s under `SectionHeader`s. Non-commerce — no PIXELS mark, no `CountKeycap`. This board introduces two flagged components — `Toggle` (the square two-position keycap-knob switch: notification prefs · privacy · the bug-logs opt-in) and `ConfirmDialog` (the centered destructive delete-account confirm, AUTH-07). The feedback row (SYS-11) only appears here; its surface is explored ×3 on the feedback-draft boards.

B1 — unverified email (AUTH-08 · soft, never a gate)

The soft-verification state (AUTH-08). Verification gates nothing in v2 — the app stays fully usable — so the prompt is an `InlineBanner` at the top of ACCOUNT, not an alarm: VERIFY YOUR EMAIL with the address echoed, the plain-words "why" (so password reset reaches a real inbox), and the RESEND EMAIL action (`POST /auth/verify-email/request`).

The EMAIL row's status flips to the orange UNVERIFIED — CHECK YOUR INBOX sub-line (vs A1's ✓ VERIFIED). One state, two surfaces — the banner to act, the row to show.

B2 — notifications · OS push declined (NOTIF-04 recovery guidance)

The recovery path for a declined OS prompt (NOTIF-04 / NOTIF-02). When push is off at the system level, per-type prefs can't deliver — so the section leads with an `InlineBanner`: PUSH IS OFF IN YOUR PHONE + the plain-words why + OPEN PHONE SETTINGS (deep-links to the OS app settings — the only place to re-grant).

The per-type `Toggle`s render their saved values but sit disabled (dimmed) — InGame keeps your choices, but they can't take effect until the OS switch is back on. No cold OS prompt is ever re-fired from here (NOTIF-04 treats the one-shot ask as precious).

B3 — blocked users (SOC-09 · list + unblock)

The blocked-users list (SOC-09) — a deeper `FlowTakeover` from the BLOCKED USERS row (FlowHeader ◂ BLOCKED). A calm note states the SOC-09 contract in plain words (mutually invisible · silent), then a `Well` of rows: avatar initials + name + BLOCKED [date] + a cream UNBLOCK keycap.

UNBLOCK is the MOD-09 terminal's lone exception — everywhere else blocked/suspended/ deleted collapse into one undisclosed "unavailable", but here you can lift your own block (`DELETE /me/blocks/:userId`). Riko · Vanta · Mossbone are the sample set. (Empty variant — "no one blocked" inviting-empty — deferred to converge.)

C1 — delete account (the new `ConfirmDialog` · AUTH-07)

The destructive confirm (§1.8 mandate; the §1.5 "confirm dialog" gap, closed). DELETE ACCOUNT summons a centered modal over a dimmed page — a square flat `scr.well` card (F-07/F-09): a red alert seal, THIS CAN'T BE UNDONE / DELETE YOUR ACCOUNT?, and the plain-words AUTH-07 ripple (collection/queue/wallet/friends/gamertags deleted; published cards unpublished but existing adopters keep their copy). Red DELETE ACCOUNT (`/destructive`) + cream CANCEL.

Flagged judgment call: this is a new spatial frame (centered modal) — the one place the app deliberately breaks its one-drawer grammar to force a stop. The alternative is to wear the same bottom-`drawer` as `ReportSheet` (CANCEL-left / commit-right). Owner picks the spatial form at the gate.

D2 — offline (writes gated · SYS-10)

Settings is read-from-cache, write-on-connection (SYS-10). The calm `OfflineStrip` rides under the header (one offline language app-wide; no red, auto-recovers). Your values still render — email, username, saved toggle positions — but every write is gated: `Toggle`s, SIGN OUT, DELETE ACCOUNT, and the verify RESEND all dim, chevrons drop on action rows.

Offline ≠ error — nothing alarms; the strip promises changes apply the moment you're back. The only red surface is the D3 `Toast`, reserved for a true write failure.

D3 — error (`LoadError` "Signal Lost" + the write-hiccup `Toast`, §1.8)

Two §1.8 error channels, one board. A load failure (the settings can't be fetched) is the full-screen `LoadError` — the dashed stepped silhouette, accent !, SIGNAL LOST, and the orange RETRY (`KeycapButton/action-alt`, the 2/4 step, F-02).

A transient write hiccup (a toggle/save didn't land) is the `Toast` sliding under the header — COULDN'T SAVE THAT CHANGE · YOUR SETTING IS UNCHANGED + orange RETRY (safe to repeat). Both reuse the converged surfaces verbatim from Store / Report — Settings introduces no new error grammar.
