S1 — Browse (the rack: rail · drop bar · grid)

The whole taxonomy is one glance: the divider rail wears every COSM-01 aisle as a keycap chip (DROPS first in gold — the value case; EFFECTS pressed + pip = you're in it), the slim drop bar keeps the seasonal case one tap away on every rack (ECON-08), and the rack itself is a dense uniform grid — ItemTile/C = full sample face (F-01) + name + type + PriceChip. Two taps from cold start to any item in the store.

The rail reuses Collection's keycap-chip grammar (same component family as the drawer's filter chips), so the Store's wayfinding is already learned. CurrencyCounter top-right → Wallet (ECON-07); COSM-02 named at the fold. Prices illustrative (OQ-002/OQ-011); lifecycle deferred.

S2 — Item detail (a sheet, not a page)

Tap a tile and detail rises as a bottom sheet — the same handle + pulled-up-tools surface as Collection's sort/filter drawer (one spatial grammar across the app, decision 0014's sheet tier). Inside: PreviewStage (HOLO live on your Destiny, full face F-01, swap line), name · type · big PriceChip, and the BuyBar as the sheet's bottom row — square gold BUY keycap (value marker; the step corner stays card-only, F-02).

S5 — Currency packs (just another rack)

Currency is a rack like any other — the same grid slot, but the tiles are PackTiles: ◈ amount big and gold, the dollar price on a cream keycap (cream = the IAP doorway; gold stays the ◈ value marker). STARTER / BEST-RATE flashes orient the tiers; ↻ RESTORE PURCHASES sits at the rack's end (ECON-06), and the earn-free line (ECON-02) keeps packs a shortcut, not a gate.

Consistency is the point in draft C: one grid, one rail, no special rooms — even money obeys the rack. Tiers illustrative (OQ-011, pairs OQ-002).

S7 — Wallet (a utility, like the rest of draft C)

The Wallet keeps the rack's plainness: a balance plate (display-21 gold — F-06's cap — + the ECON-01 one-liner + BUY ◈), then the ledger as dense utility rows — LedgerRow/C = signed badge · what · when · amount, the full ECON-07 narrative (−8 EMBER · +30 pack · −1 adoption · +1 login · +5 grant), newest first, every row auditable.

The fragment is the ECON-09 negative variant: −3 ◈ alert-red on the plate, the ⊘ reversal row, no-clawback reassurance, BUY ◈ as the bridge. The header counter is the door in from everywhere; here the plate replaces it.

S8 — Ownership (states in the grid; the earned sub-rack)

The rack wears its entitlement states in place of the price slot: OwnedTag (cream ✓ + "in your editor" — the price never returns) · LockedTag (padlock + "returns with its drop", tile desaturated — a date, never a price; ECON-08's return hook) · the normal PriceChip default as seen across S1. One slot, one meaning at a time — scannable at grid speed.

EARNED ONLY is a gold-edged sub-rack pinned at the category's end: achievement exclusives with EarnedOnlyTag (gold-bordered, gold type — outlined, never gold-filled, so it can't read as a price) + the achievement that wins each. Visible, coveted, never purchasable (COSM-04 — owner-default treatment, flagged at the gate).
