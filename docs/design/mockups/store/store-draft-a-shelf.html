S5 — Currency packs (the till shelf)

The CURRENCY aisle is the only place dollars exist: four PackTiles — ◈ amount big and gold, the dollar price on a cream keycap (deliberately not gold: gold marks ◈ value, cream marks the IAP doorway). STARTER and BEST-RATE flashes carry the tier story. Tapping a dollar keycap opens the native payment sheet; the grant lands after server receipt validation (ECON-06/RevenueCat), and ↻ RESTORE PURCHASES sits quiet at the bottom.

The dashed sign keeps the economy honest: ◈ is earnable free (+1 login bonus, milestones, ECON-02) — packs are a shortcut, not a paywall. Tiers/prices illustrative (OQ-002/OQ-011).

S6 — Purchase states (success · failure Toast · restore)

Success: the IAP sheet closes and the shop answers in its own voice — a gold +30 tick chip beside the glowing CurrencyCounter (5 ➞ 35), a ✓ strip naming the receipt check, the bought pack stamped on its tile. Deliberately quiet (§1.1 conventional commerce; the OQ-040 ritual stays with cards).

Failure proposes the catalog's missing Toast: a service-bell strip under the header — what failed, the reassurance ("nothing charged"), and an orange RETRY keycap. Restore is the third fragment: re-check receipts, re-sync entitlements, state "all caught up."

S7 — Wallet (the till + the receipt, incl. the negative variant)

The Wallet is the shop's till: a dark readout plate with the balance in gold at display scale (21px — F-06's ceiling, prominence without breaking the type law), BUY ◈ beside the one-wallet explainer (ECON-01's two spend types in one line). The ledger is a cream paper receipt with perforated edges — LedgerRow = sign · what · when · amount, newest first: the full ECON-07 narrative (+5 grant · +1 login · −1 adoption · +30 pack · −8 EMBER).

The fragment is the ECON-09 negative variant: a refund reversed 30 ◈ → −3 ◈ in alert red, the ⊘ reversal row, plain-words recovery copy, and BUY ◈ as the bridge back. Reached from every screen via the header counter; on the Wallet itself the counter yields to the readout (no double display).

S8 — Ownership (owned · locked · the trophy case)

Three entitlement states on one shelf: OwnedTag (cream keycap ✓ — the price chip is gone for good; the shelf-edge label points to the editors where owned items are used) · a normal priced neighbour · LockedTag (padlock + “NEXT DROP” — desaturated, keeps a date, never a price; the ECON-08 return hook).

EARNED ONLY = the glass trophy case (COSM-04): gold-bordered, glass-sheen, items sit on plinths with EarnedOnlyTag (gold-bordered, gold type — outlined, never gold-filled, so it can't be misread as a price) and the achievement that wins them. Visible, coveted, never purchasable — flagged at the gate as the owner-default treatment.
