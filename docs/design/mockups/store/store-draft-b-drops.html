S8 — Ownership (a category page wearing its states)

An index category page (EFFECTS) carrying all the entitlement states inline: OwnedTag (cream ✓, price gone for good, "in your editor" pointer) · a normal PriceChip row · LockedTag (padlock, desaturated, "returns with its drop" — a date, never a price; the ECON-08 hook).
