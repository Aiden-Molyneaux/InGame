Welcome & Auth (§4.13) — DRAFT B · games-forward landing

DRAFT B leads with the GAMES, not a mini-device. The alternative direction to the current board (which leads with an on-screen mini-DEVICE hero). The W1/W1b landing now sells what InGame IS — a player-built catalog of games and custom cards — right from the logged-out front door: a showcase of real community-built `GameCard`s (faces never cropped, F-01), two system-wide stat readouts (total games in the catalog + total hours logged across all players — framed as community aggregates, NOT the visitor's own), and a contribution-inviting tagline that makes the visitor feel invited to add their games and design their cards. Everything else is UNCHANGED from the source board — the sign-in-first form still rides below the hero on the landing, and W2…W11 (create-account, username availability, SIWA linking, password reset, verify-email, Signal Lost, Offline) plus the grayed/locked NavBand are carried verbatim. No on-screen gold (F-02): the stats, showcase and tagline are informational, not acquisitive; every auth CTA stays orange/cream. No pink on any screen surface (F-05) — pink is the physical shell LED only. Square on-screen chrome (F-07), F-06 type only (21/15/11/9), one font per surface (F-08). Composed from the §1.5 catalog.

W11 — offline (writes gated · SYS-10)

Offline ≠ error (SYS-10). Auth is inherently a write (it can't read from cache), so the calm `OfflineStrip` rides under the header (one offline language app-wide; no red, auto-recovers) and every CTA gates — SIGN IN, the Apple button, and FORGOT PASSWORD all dim. Typed values are kept; a plain line promises it resumes on reconnect. The only red on this board is W4's field errors and W5's auth-fail.
